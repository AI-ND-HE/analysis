CHANGES MADE:
The CSV file "dt025-table-5.csv" is the Table 5 that has been downloaded on 12 October 2026 at 22:18 (GMT+1) from this reference:
Table 5—HE academic staff by disability and academic employment function 2014/15 to 2024/25 | HESA. (2026, February). https://www.hesa.ac.uk/data-and-analysis/staff/table-5

The Table 5 CSV file has been modified to define "neurodiversity related disability" as the following three categories:
1. Learning difference such as dyslexia, dyspraxia or AD(H)D 
2. Social/communication conditions such as a speech and language impairment or an autistic spectrum condition 
3. Development condition that you have had since childhood which affects motor, cognitive, social and emotional skills, and speech and language 

This adds up to 5,600 total staff (~2.3% when rounded to 1 decimal place) out of 244,755 total academic staff in Table 5, which is what has been cited in our paper.
