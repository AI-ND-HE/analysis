CHANGES MADE:
For your information, I believe there used to be a fourth Qualtrics survey for collecting email addresses from non-Prolific participants who wanted to take part in follow-up studies. This survey was separate from the main survey (i.e. "AIND_Survey.qsf") for non-Prolific participants. However, when the email addresses were permanently deleted to meet our ethics and GDPR requirements, I believe that survey may also have been deleted. That is my best guess anyway, as I can no longer find that fourth survey on Qualtrics. The "AIND_Survey_Prolific_Emails.qsf" file is similar enough to be a good placeholder and shows what that survey would have largely looked like.
